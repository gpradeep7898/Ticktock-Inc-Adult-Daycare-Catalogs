How to use Tick Tock Catalogs

Every day

  1. Change a price: open the catalog, click the product, type the new price, Save. The same item # updates in every catalog and on the website list, and the catalog's "prices as of" date becomes today.
  2. Add a product: click the dashed + Add product box at the end of the sheet (or Add product at the top). Fill item #, name, pack, price, add a photo, choose where it goes, Save.
  3. Two products in one box: open the second product and turn on Share a box with the product before. They print side by side, like Bic Classic and Sensitive.
  4. Hide or remove: turn off Show in PDF to hide it but keep it for later. Use Delete only when it is gone for good.
  5. Reorder: drag a box by its corner handle, or use Move earlier / later in the product panel.
  6. Many prices at once: Change prices raises or lowers a whole catalog by a percent or dollar amount, with a preview first.
  7. Made a mistake? Click Undo on the message at the bottom right after a change.

Getting files out

  1. Download PDF (top right of each catalog) gives that one catalog. In the blue Send to customers bar, Full catalog, one PDF gives every catalog in one file, and All 25 PDFs (.zip) gives them as separate files.
  2. Website list → Download website sheet gives ticktockinc.com.xlsx in the same format as before. Upload it to the website as usual.

Sending to customers on WhatsApp

  1. Keep File size on Small, for WhatsApp in the blue Send to customers bar at the top of the Catalogs tab. Photos still look sharp on a phone and the files are a fraction of the old size. Switch to Large only for printing.
  2. Click Full catalog, one PDF. You get one file with every catalog and a contents page; customers tap a category to jump to it.
  3. Send that one file on WhatsApp, or a single catalog's PDF when a customer asks for one category.
  4. To reach many customers at once, use a broadcast list in the WhatsApp Business app (up to 256 people per list; they must have your number saved).

Using it as a team

  1. Use the Share button on this page to invite people from the company.
  2. People with edit access can change prices and photos. People with view access can search, look up prices on a call and download PDFs, but cannot change anything.
  3. Everyone sees changes live. Recent changes shows who changed what and when.
  4. Keep one person in charge of uploading the website sheet so the site and the catalogs stay the same.
